What Is Actually Happening

Your Post Gets Attention.
But the Parent Is Still Unsure.

A parent sees your poster, asks about fees and class timing, then stops replying. It does not always mean the centre is not good. They may still be unsure whether the programme, teacher, level, teaching method, and environment are right for their child.

Before a parent trusts you with their child, they need more than “Registration Open” and a fee list. They need confidence in the programme.

01

They See the Poster

A subject, exam level, intake announcement, or student result catches their attention.

02

They Still Do Not Know Whether It Fits

There is no clear teaching approach, teacher context, programme explanation, or indication of whether the class suits their child.

03

WhatsApp Starts With “Fees How Much?”

Your team repeatedly answers the same basic questions about price, timing, level, location, and available classes.

04

The Interested Parent Goes Quiet

The enquiry remains inside the chat list, or the parent starts comparing several other centres.

Why Interested Parents Go Quiet

The Problem Is Not Always the Programme.
Sometimes It Is the Enquiry Journey.

01

No Confidence-Building Step

Parents go from seeing an advertisement directly into WhatsApp without properly understanding the centre.

02

Repetitive Questions

Staff repeatedly answer questions about fees, class times, location, age groups, levels, and available subjects.

03

The Centre Looks Similar to Competitors

When the programme is not explained clearly, parents compare centres mainly by price and convenience.

04

Slow Response Time

Parents may contact several tuition centres and continue the conversation with whichever centre responds clearly and quickly.

05

No Clear Enquiry Organisation

New enquiries, trial-class requests, follow-ups, and inactive leads become mixed together.

06

Inconsistent Follow-Up

Interested parents are forgotten because staff are busy teaching, managing classes, and handling daily operations.

The Journey

What Happens After a Parent Clicks Your Advertisement

Stage 1

Targeted Advertisement

The parent sees a message relevant to their child’s subject, level, exam, learning gap, or desired result.

Stage 2

Focused Landing Page

The parent understands what the centre teaches, who the programme is for, the teaching approach, and what the next step will be.

Stage 3

Parent Enquiry Form

Useful parent and student details are captured up front, before anyone opens WhatsApp.

Stage 4

Enquiry Enters the Follow-Up System

The enquiry is recorded, categorised, and assigned for follow-up.

Stage 5

Trial Class or Assessment

The team contacts the parent with useful context rather than beginning with the same basic questions.

Stage 6

Enrolment Follow-Up

The centre tracks attendance, parent feedback, programme suitability, and the enrolment decision.
