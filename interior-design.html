What Is Actually Happening

Your Advertisement Gets Attention.
Then the Trust Disappears.

A homeowner sees one attractive kitchen, bedroom, condominium, or landed-home renovation, clicks the advertisement, and lands directly in WhatsApp with almost no context.

Your team asks several questions. The homeowner asks for a rough price. The conversation becomes slow, unclear, or repetitive. Eventually, the prospect stops replying.

You are asking a stranger to trust your company with their entire home — and possibly RM50,000, RM100,000, or more — after seeing only one advertisement. That is too big a jump.

Why Good Leads Go Cold

The Problem Is Not Always Lead Quality.
Sometimes It Is the Journey.

01

No Trust-Building Step

Prospects go from an advertisement directly into a sales conversation without properly understanding your company.

02

Incomplete Project Information

Your team has to manually ask about location, property type, budget, renovation scope, and timeline.

03

Price Becomes the Main Conversation

When homeowners do not understand your process or value, they compare companies mainly by price.

04

Slow Response Time

Good prospects may contact several interior design companies and respond to whichever company handles them first.

05

No Clear Lead Organisation

Serious projects, early-stage enquiries, and unsuitable prospects are mixed together.

06

Inconsistent Follow-Up

Potential clients are forgotten because the team is busy handling existing projects.
